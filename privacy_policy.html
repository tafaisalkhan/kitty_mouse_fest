Kitty Hunt Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Kitty Hunt saves level progress, stars, coins, sound and accessibility preferences, unlocked items, and ad removal status in local app storage on your device. Kitty Hunt does not run its own account or game data server.

Advertising

When advertising is enabled in an Android release build, Google AdMob may serve banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads. AdMob may process device identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, ad views, interactions, and diagnostics under Google's advertising policies. When ads are unavailable or disabled, no ads are shown.

Purchases

When Google Play purchases are enabled in a release build, Google Play handles payment and purchase verification for Remove Ads, coin packs, and support purchases. Kitty Hunt stores only the resulting game entitlement or coin balance locally and does not receive payment card details.

Data Control

You can erase locally saved game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Google's handling of ad and purchase data follows its own retention and deletion policies.

Kitty Hunt does not ask players to create an account or enter personal information. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of ads and purchases.
